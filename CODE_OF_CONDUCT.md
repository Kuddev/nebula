# Code of Conduct

Pebrel welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Participation

- Treat other people with respect. Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks.
- Give constructive feedback and make space for questions and differing perspectives.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or publish private information about others.
- Keep issues, pull requests, and project discussions relevant and free of spam.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **fickleheartedkeys@163.com**. Include links
and enough context for the maintainers to review the situation. Do not include
unrelated private information.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation when needed
to protect project discussions. Reports will be handled with respect for the
privacy of those involved.

This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces administered by the Pebrel maintainers.
